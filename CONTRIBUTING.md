## 2. Contribution Methods

Contributions may be submitted through the following methods, where enabled by the repository owner:

- Bug reports and issue submissions.
- Feature requests and technical suggestions.
- Code contributions through pull requests.
- Documentation improvements.
- Mathematical, scientific, algorithmic, or numerical-solver suggestions.
- Testing, benchmarking, and performance reports.

The project maintainer reserves the right to accept, reject, modify, or request revisions to any contribution.

## 3. Contributor Rights and Ownership

Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

- Contributors retain copyright ownership of their original contributions to the extent provided by applicable law.
- Contributors must have the legal right to submit any material they contribute.
- Contributors must not submit material that infringes third-party intellectual property rights.
- Contributors must identify relevant third-party code, libraries, datasets, or other externally licensed materials.

**Submission of a contribution does not automatically transfer copyright ownership to the project maintainer.**

This permission is intended to allow the maintainer to incorporate and maintain contributions within the proprietary project.

It does not grant contributors or other third parties permission to redistribute or commercially exploit the proprietary project itself.

## 4. Code and Documentation Standards

Contributions should:

- Be readable, maintainable, and reasonably documented.
- Follow the existing project architecture and coding conventions.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Include appropriate error handling.
- Preserve existing functionality unless an intentional change is documented.
- Include tests or reproducible validation where reasonably applicable.

For mathematical, scientific, solver, and simulation-related contributions:

- Clearly document assumptions and limitations.
- Identify relevant equations, derivations, and references.
- Specify units and dimensional conventions.
- Explain numerical approximations, stability considerations, and computational limitations.
- Avoid presenting unverified results as established scientific facts.

## 5. Third-Party Materials

Contributors must not introduce third-party materials into the project without verifying that their applicable licenses permit the intended use.

Any externally sourced material must be appropriately identified and attributed.

Third-party licenses remain applicable to their respective components and are not superseded by this project's proprietary license.

## 6. Pull Request Requirements

Before submitting a pull request:

1. Verify that your changes work as intended.
2. Describe the purpose and scope of the contribution.
3. Document significant implementation or mathematical decisions.
4. Identify new dependencies.
5. Include relevant tests or validation results.
6. Confirm that you have the necessary rights to submit the contributed material.

Pull requests may be reviewed, revised, rejected, or closed at the maintainer's discretion.

## 7. Confidentiality and Security

Do not submit:

- Passwords, API keys, access tokens, or other credentials.
- Private personal information.
- Confidential third-party materials.
- Proprietary information belonging to another individual or organization without authorization.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately through an appropriate channel rather than publicly disclosed before the maintainer has had an opportunity to investigate them.

## 8. No Automatic Employment or Partnership

Contributing to this project does not establish an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, agency relationship, or entitlement to compensation.

Any such arrangement must be established separately through an explicit written agreement.

## 9. Changes to These Guidelines

The project maintainer reserves the right to update these guidelines. Continued submission of contributions after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms for those future contributions.
